How the noise was measured: most days, I recorded about an hour of video through my window, with a decibel reader running at the same time. I saved a clip when a car engine was audible in the video and either the decibel reader crossed 40 dB while it was audible and annoying, or I'd flagged in the moment that I could hear a vehicle and it bothered me. Clips are posted on my YouTube channel, @Zella-U. The "estimated minutes/hours" figures scale that hour's rate up to a full day. Loud-moment counts are never scaled — they're the exact number I tagged by hand. The day-by-day chart, top numbers, and three loud moments cover the past 12 months; the day-of-week and month-over-month charts use every day logged so far.

How the calls were logged: every time I called Seattle police's non-emergency line to report the noise, I logged the call, whether an officer ever responded, how long that took, and whether they found the vehicle.

This page reads its data live from noise-data.csv and police-data.csv in the same folder as this page. To update it, replace those files with new exports in the same format — no need to touch this page itself.
